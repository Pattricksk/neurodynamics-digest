Cortical travelling waves: mechanisms and computational principles

Muller L, Sejnowski TJ
Nature Reviews Neuroscience · 2018-03-22 · doi:10.1038/nrn.2018.20
Pre-clinicalPeer reviewedmultielectrode recordingsnetwork theory#travelling waves#catch-up

The review that pulled together evidence for travelling waves at mesoscopic (single-area) and macroscopic (whole-brain) scales, and placed it within theory of how recurrent networks generate and propagate waves. It argues waves transiently modulate spiking and excitability as they pass and may serve functions from memory consolidation in sleep to processing dynamic visual input in the awake cortex. Why it mattered: it gave the field a shared vocabulary and a mechanistic framing (waves as a computational feature, not just a phase artefact), which much of the later EEG/MEG travelling-wave literature builds on.

CAVEATS A review, mostly built on invasive animal recordings; it says less about how to measure waves robustly at the scalp.
