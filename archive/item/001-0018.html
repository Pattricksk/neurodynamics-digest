Brain dynamics exhibit scale-dependent reversibility during consciousness transitions

Nanyin Zhang, Xu Han, Xiaoai Chen, Samuel Cramer, Yizhuo Ding
Research Square (preprint) · 2026-10-27 · doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-11194430/v1
PropofolfMRIPre-clinicalLFPfMRIscale-dependent analysisanaesthesia#anaesthesia#reversibility#travelling waves

Rats under graded propofol were recorded with simultaneous local electrophysiology, whole-brain fMRI and pupillometry. LFPs, regional BOLD and pairwise FC changed largely reversibly between loss and recovery of consciousness.

CAVEATS Preprint, rodents, one anaesthetic; sample and effect sizes aren't in the abstract.
