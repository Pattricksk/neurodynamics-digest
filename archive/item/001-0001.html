Sustained antidepressant-like effects of psychedelics through 5-HT2AR–β-arrestin signaling

Licong He, Lingjie Tang, Dongmei Cao, Wenwen Duan, Jing Yu, Pei Zhang, Huiqiong Li, Yumeng Wang, Bing Meng, Huan Wang, Yue Zhou, Wenhao Li, Xiaoyu Li, Zhijie Liu, Lei Zhang, Jianjun Cheng, Sheng Wang
Nature Neuroscience · 2026-10-23 · doi:10.1038/s41593-026-02497-5
Non-hal (5-HT2A agonists)Pre-clinicalbiased agonismbehaviourmice#5-HT2A#beta-arrestin#non-hallucinogenic antidepressant

Using structure-guided 5-HT2AR–β-arrestin-biased agonists, the authors argue that β-arrestin signalling, not Gαq, drives prolonged antidepressant-like effects in mice. The key is kinetics: LSD dissociates slowly, so repeated low doses can accumulate receptor engagement close to a single high dose without the peak, sustaining β-arrestin signalling while limiting Gαq-mediated Ca²⁺ responses.

CAVEATS Animal behaviour stands in for both mood and subjective effects, and the absence of behavioural disruption doesn't mean the absence of altered experience. Funded by Chinese state and Shanghai sources (MOST, NSFC, CAS, ShanghaiTech). Several authors have filed patents on the new 5-HT2AR agonists through their universities (declared in the competing-interests statement), so the biased-agonist claims come from an interested party.
