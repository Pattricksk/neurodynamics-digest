Where does EEG come from and what does it mean?

Cohen MX
Trends in Neurosciences · 2017-04 · doi:10.1016/j.tins.2017.02.004
EEGClinicalPeer reviewedEEGoscillations#EEG generators#catch-up

A short, provocative opinion piece arguing that we know 'shockingly little' about where EEG signals come from and what they mean. It sets out what is known, why the answers matter, and how modern circuit tools (measuring and manipulating neurons with high spatiotemporal precision) might finally provide answers, proposing oscillations as the best anchor because they're seen across scales and species. Why it mattered: it became a widely cited framing for the generators problem and a call to link scalp EEG to circuit mechanisms.

CAVEATS An opinion piece, not new data; much of the cross-scale work it called for has appeared since.
