Quiet breathing evokes scalp EEG potentials in wakefulness and in every sleep stage

Grassion L, Andrillon T, Similowski T
Journal of Physiology · 2026-10-28 · doi:10.1113/jp290737
EEGpeer reviewedpolysomnography EEGrespiration-locked averagingadults (Cleveland Family Study)#brain-body coupling#respiration#sleep

Using Cleveland Family Study polysomnography from 729 participants (median 7,871 respiratory cycles each), the authors averaged EEG time-locked to inspiration onset. They found a pre-inspiratory component about 300 ms before inspiration and post-inspiratory peaks up to 3 s after. The potentials persisted in N2, N3 and REM sleep with a simplified, K-complex-like shape, alongside more spindle-range activity. They were present regardless of BMI and unchanged in the 203 participants without sleep-disordered breathing.

CAVEATS What these potentials mean physiologically or clinically is still unknown, and scalp EEG can't say where they come from. Polysomnography montages are sparse.
