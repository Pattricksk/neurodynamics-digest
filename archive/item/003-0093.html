Attention travels as a wave at the rhythm of whichever sense is cueing it

Kong Y, Dugué L
bioRxiv (preprint) · 2026-11-05 · doi:10.64898/2026.11.04.763906
EEGHealthy volunteersClinicalPreprintEEGtravelling waveshealthy adults#travelling waves#attention#alpha#theta

Alpha lateralisation is a standard marker of sustained visuospatial attention, and anterior-to-posterior alpha travelling waves had recently been shown to lateralise too. The question here: are the waves top-down attention signals or a by-product of attending to visual input? The authors cued attention through different senses (auditory cueing vs visual stimulation) while keeping a visual discrimination task. With auditory cueing, lateralised travelling waves appeared in theta; with visual stimulation, in alpha.
