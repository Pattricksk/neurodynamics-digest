Psilocybin, cognitive stability and drinking in AUD with depression (medRxiv, 29 Oct).

Sayalı C, Barrett FS
medRxiv (preprint) · 2026-10-29 · doi:10.64898/2026.10.27.26366630
PsilocybinDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)Alcohol use disorderClinicalPreprintcognitive tasksBayesian analysisadults with AUD and depression#psychedelic addiction#cognitive control#alcohol

Sayalı … Barrett. Secondary analysis of an RCT (12 psilocybin, 13 placebo): psilocybin slowed cued task switching, but most of 34 cognitive measures were inconclusive and none survived FDR. Drinking fell in both arms, and shifts toward stability tracked drinking reduction. Small and exploratory.
