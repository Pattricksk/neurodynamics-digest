Altered brain-body activity during emotional mental imagery in major depressive disorder

Alna Reem Al Latheef, Vera Flasbeck, Georg Juckel
Scientific Reports · 2026-10-24 · doi:10.1038/s41598-026-75968-1
EEGDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)ClinicalEEG + ECGHRVadults with MDDcontrols#brain-body#depression

37 controls and 35 patients with MDD did an emotional imagery paradigm with simultaneous EEG and ECG. Patients differed in alpha, gamma and delta activity (not ERPs), linked to worse cognition and symptoms, and HRV pointed to higher sympathetic activity.

CAVEATS Modest sample, cross-sectional, and coupling assessed by correlation rather than directional measures. Medication status isn't reported in the abstract.
