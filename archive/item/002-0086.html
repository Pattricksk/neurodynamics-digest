Psilocybin tilts learning toward rewarded outcomes in rat prefrontal cortex

Jacobs D, Moghaddam B
eLife (reviewed preprint) · 2026-10-30 · doi:10.7554/elife.112830.1
PsilocybinGamblingPre-clinicalReviewed preprintsingle-unit recordingsreinforcement learning modelsrats#reward learning#uncertainty#gambling-relevant

In a value-based probabilistic choice task with computational modelling and mPFC single-unit recordings, psilocybin improved choice under uncertainty and shifted learning rates bidirectionally: more updating from rewarded actions, less from unrewarded ones. Neural encoding of rewarded outcomes strengthened and of unrewarded outcomes weakened.
