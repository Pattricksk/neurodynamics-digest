Psilocybin and nucleus accumbens inhibition via astrocytes (Exp Mol Med, 5 Nov).

Kang SM, Koo JW

Kang, … Koo. In rodents, psilocybin-like tryptamines raised NAc activity while lowering extracellular GABA; psilocin reduced tonic (not miniature) GABA currents in medium spiny neurons; psilocybin lowered astrocytic GABA and raised astrocytic NFATc4/ALDH5A1, with 5-HT2A antagonism blunting astrocyte calcium responses. Mesolimbic tone is relevant to the reward circuitry in your addiction work.
