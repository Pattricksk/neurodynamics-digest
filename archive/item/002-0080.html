The same E/I input shift gives different aperiodic slopes depending on circuit wiring

Cai Z, Ming D
International Journal of Neural Systems · 2026-10-30 · doi:10.1142/s012906572750033x
PropofolPre-clinicalPeer reviewedconnectome-constrained modellingECoGthalamic recordingsaperiodic exponentjuvenile pigssimulated microcircuits#E/I balance#aperiodic slope#criticality

The authors embedded electron-microscopy-reconstructed cortical microcircuits in near-critical dynamics and generated field potentials with fixed synaptic, membrane and forward-model parameters. The relationship between E/I input and the aperiodic exponent differed between biological and topology-randomised networks (a significant E/I × topology interaction). In paired cortical ECoG and thalamic recordings from juvenile pigs under propofol, exponent changes were larger in thalamus than cortex, even after accounting for global state, baseline spectra and distance from criticality.

CAVEATS A model-plus-animal study; the pig recordings are under anaesthesia, and the claim is about interpretation, not a new estimator.
