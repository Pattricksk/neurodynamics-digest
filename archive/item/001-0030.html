Investigation of the pharmacological and circuit basis for psychedelic drug effects on interval timing in mice

Landon M. Klein, Jared Kopelman, Richard F. Sharp, Susan B. Powell, Mark A. Geyer, Jared W. Young, Adam L. Halberstadt
Psychopharmacology · 2026-10-19 · doi:10.1007/s00213-026-07181-x
Psilocybin (psilocin)Pre-clinicalinterval timing task5-HT2A pharmacologymice#time perception#mPFC

Male C57BL/6J mice judged whether a light lasted shorter or longer than 6.5 s. Psilocin (0.8 mg/kg SC) altered timing accuracy and precision; the 5-HT2A antagonist M100907 blocked this, the 5-HT1A antagonist WAY-100,635 didn't.

CAVEATS Male mice only, one psilocin dose, and a task far from human subjective time. No competing interests declared (NIDA funded).
