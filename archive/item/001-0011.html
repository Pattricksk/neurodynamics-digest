Sex Differences in Acute Responses to Psychedelics: Evidence for Greater Subjective Intensity and Impairment in Female Participants

Natasha L Mason, Eline CHM Haijen-Bongers, Kim PC Kuypers, Aline Frick, Stefan W Toennes, Pablo Mallaroni, Johannes G Ramaekers
bioRxiv (preprint) · 2026-07-13 · doi:10.64898/2026.07.08.737179
Psychedelics (general)Healthy volunteersClinicalpooled analysishealthy volunteers#sex differences#acute effects

Mason … Ramaekers. Pooled placebo-controlled data (94 drug observations, 51 female; psilocybin, 2C-B, LSD): women reported stronger effects on five measures (medium-to-large), with no difference in peak drug levels.
