EigenBrain: which receptor maps can be approximated from MRI eigenmodes (preprint, 2 Nov).

Wan B, Kirschner M
bioRxiv (preprint) · 2026-11-02 · doi:10.64898/2026.10.27.758642
fMRIPsychosis / schizophreniaHealthy volunteersClinicalPreprintMRI eigenmodesreceptor mapsspatial reconstructionhealthy adultsautismschizophrenia#chemoarchitecture#eigenmodes#receptor maps

Wan, … Valk & Kirschner, with Margulies and Hansen among the authors. Of 23 molecular maps, eight (serotonin, dopamine, histamine, cholinergic and opioid markers) could be approximated from functional, but not structural or geometric, eigenmodes, then applied to autism and schizophrenia cohorts. Relevant to receptor-informed analyses of psychedelic fMRI, and to spatial autocorrelation (see Catch-up).
