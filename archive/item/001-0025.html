Theta-Band Temporal Interference Stimulation Modulates Food Reward Prediction Error Encoding in Individuals with Elevated Food Addiction Symptoms

Yatong Wen, Mo Wang, Xinwen Dong, Mateusz Gola, Quanying Liu, Yiheng Tu, Yonghui Li
Neuroscience Bulletin · 2026-10-20 · doi:10.1007/s12264-026-01738-8
EEGFood addictionClinicaltemporal interference stimulationFRNcomputational modellingpeople with elevated food-addiction symptoms#food addiction#prediction error#brain stimulation

A single-blind, sham-controlled pre–post study in people with elevated food-addiction symptoms, who did a food incentive-delay task with EEG during theta-band TI stimulation targeting a dACC-centred network.

CAVEATS Single-blind, N = 34, with key effects right at p ≈ .05, so preliminary. Numbers are from the preprint; the journal version is paywalled and I couldn't check whether they changed. 'Food addiction' is a symptom-scale definition, not a diagnosis. No competing interests declared. Full text paywalled; numbers from the preprint.
