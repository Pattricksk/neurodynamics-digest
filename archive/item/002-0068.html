Temporal interference in mouse cortex: the kilohertz carrier alone activates neurons, and the envelope organises rhythm

Yoshimoto R, Gutekunst C-A
bioRxiv (preprint) · 2026-10-21 · doi:10.64898/2026.10.15.759497
preprinttwo-photon imagingtemporal interference stimulationmodellingmice#brain stimulation#TI

Two-photon imaging in mice, immunohistochemistry and modelling showed that kilohertz stimulation without amplitude modulation evoked widespread activity. The amplitude modulation organised rhythmic responses at the modulation frequency, strongest near the interference centre.

CAVEATS Preprint in mice; field strengths and geometry that may not translate to human scalp TI.
