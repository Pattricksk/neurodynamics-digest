Alcohol cues, skin conductance and the pgACC, by sex (Addiction Biology, 3 Nov).

Chen Y, Li C-SR
Addiction Biology · 2026-11-03 · doi:10.1111/adb.70197
AlcoholfMRIClinicalPeer reviewedtask fMRIskin conductanceadult drinkers#addiction neuroimaging#cue reactivity#sex differences#brain-body coupling

Chen, … Li (Yale). 94 adult drinkers, fMRI with skin conductance during alcohol vs neutral cues. Females had larger cue SCRs, but SCR tracked AUDIT scores only in males, as did a pgACC cluster whose cue response correlated with SCR. A small example of adding a peripheral arousal channel to cue reactivity, close to your brain–body set-up.
