Sex-related differences in outcomes following psilocybin or escitalopram: a Bayesian secondary analysis

Aline Frick, Grace Blest-Hopley, Manesh Girn, David Erritzoe, David Nutt, Robin Carhart-Harris
Psychopharmacology · 2026-10-26 · doi:10.1007/s00213-026-07177-7
PsilocybinEscitalopramDepression (incl. TRD/MDD)ClinicalBayesian reanalysisadults with depression#sex differences#Imperial trial

A post hoc Bayesian secondary analysis of the six-week double-blind psilocybin vs escitalopram RCT (N = 59, 20 female; psilocybin n = 30 with 11 female, escitalopram n = 29 with 9 female). There was no overall sex difference on any outcome, and none on the depression scales.

CAVEATS A secondary analysis of a trial that wasn't powered for sex effects, with 9–11 women per arm. Results are prior-sensitive, so treat them as hypothesis-generating.
