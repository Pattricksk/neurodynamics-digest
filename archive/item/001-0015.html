Language-associated brain representations covary with disruption of selfhood under psilocybin

Greg Cooper, Devon Stoliker, Vincent Sebastian Astl Delgado, George Blackburne, Leonardo Novelli, David W. Green, Ravi Das, Adeel Razi, Jeremy I. Skipper
bioRxiv (preprint) · 2026-10-14 · doi:10.64898/2026.10.06.757069
Psychedelics (general)fMRIHealthy volunteersClinicalinter-subject similaritynaturalistic fMRIhealthy volunteers#language network#ego dissolution

Participants heard music and watched moving clouds in the scanner at baseline and after 19 mg psilocybin. The authors tested whether inter-subject similarity of brain activity increased with each pair's summed Ego Dissolution Inventory (EDI) score (N = 43), using elementary visual imagery (N = 49) and baseline as comparators.

CAVEATS Preprint. The brain–EDI correlation only just survives correction, and decoding with Neurosynth maps is a reverse inference.
