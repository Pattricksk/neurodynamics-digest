Neurite Complexity and Plasticity Markers Track Synaptic Vesicle Density in the Living Human Brain: A Multimodal PET-MRI-EEG Study

18 healthy volunteers underwent [11C]-UCB-J PET (SV2A synaptic density), diffusion MRI with NODDI, resting-state fMRI and EEG plasticity tasks (roving mismatch negativity and visual LTP), before and after ketamine (n = 11) or DMT (n = 7).

CAVEATS Very small groups (11 and 7), so 'no significant change' isn't 'no change'. Healthy volunteers, one 1–8 day window. Preprint.
