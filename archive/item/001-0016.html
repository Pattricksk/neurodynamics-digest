Serotonergic psychedelics delay resting state neurovascular coupling dynamics

Lautaro Mundel, Vicente Medel, Christopher Timmermann, Patricio Orio, Josephine Cruzat, Carla Pallavicini, Gustavo Deco, Agustín Ibáñez, Robin Carhart-Harris, Yonatan Sanz Perl, Enzo Tagliazucchi, Enzo Tagliazucchi
bioRxiv (preprint) · 2026-10-16 · doi:10.64898/2026.10.09.758082
LSDDMTfMRIHealthy volunteersClinicalHRF modellinghealthy volunteers#haemodynamic confound#methods

Blind deconvolution of resting BOLD showed that both LSD and DMT increased estimated haemodynamic delays, in regions overlapping serotonin receptors involved in both psychedelic action and vascular regulation.

CAVEATS Preprint. Haemodynamic delay is estimated, not directly measured, and sample sizes aren't in the abstract.
