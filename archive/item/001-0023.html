Effect of dulaglutide on functional and structural MRI changes during cigarette smoking cessation

Clara O Sailer, Sophia Lengsfeld, David Coynel, Davide Zanchi, Nica Jeanloz, Fabienne Baur, Cemile Bathelt, Katja Bologna, Tanja Vukajlovic, Sandrine A Urwyler, Mirjam Christ-Crain, Bettina Winzeler
Brain Communications · 2026-10-27 · doi:10.1093/braincomms/fcag410
DulaglutideVareniclinefMRINicotine / smokingClinicalRCT substudysmokers#nicotine#GLP-1#addiction neuroimaging

A predefined imaging substudy of an RCT (parent n = 255): 71 smokers were randomised to 1.5 mg dulaglutide (n = 35) or placebo (n = 36) for 12 weeks on top of varenicline and counselling, with structural and functional MRI at baseline and week 12 (59 completed).

CAVEATS An add-on design against strong standard care, so a ceiling effect is plausible. Modest sample size for a between-group imaging comparison.
