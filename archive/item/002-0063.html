The authors extend the convolution-based canonical microcircuit (four populations: spiny stellate, superficial pyramidal, inhibitory interneurons, deep pyramidal) with two global (across-region) excitability parameters, modelled as self-inhibition: one for pyramidal (E) cells and one for inhibitory (I) cells, plus condition-specific modulations of each. CAVEATS The patient-like effects are simulations; no patient data were fitted. I-cell recovery is only fair and test–retest reliability is unknown. The E self-inhibition parameter might partly reflect a PV interneuron loop rather than pyramidal cells themselves. Code and empirical priors are public (SPM12 r7771, MATLAB).
