HRV biofeedback through a spectral DCM lens (Appl Psychophysiol Biofeedback, 3 Nov).

Ma L, Moeller FG
Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback · 2026-11-03 · doi:10.1007/s10484-026-09818-1
fMRIHealthy volunteersClinicalPeer reviewedresting-state fMRIspectral DCMPEBHRVhealthy adults#brain-body coupling#HRV biofeedback#effective connectivity#breathing

Ma, … Moeller. Secondary analysis of an open dataset: 8 weeks of HRV biofeedback (n = 15) vs a video-game control (n = 17), resting fMRI with spectral DCM and PEB. Biofeedback increased mPFC→ACC and mPFC→thalamus effective connectivity and reduced right hippocampus→VLPFC, the latter linked to SDNN change. Small groups, but a worked example of DCM-PEB on a breathing intervention.
