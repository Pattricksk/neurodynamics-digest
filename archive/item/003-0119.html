A multilevel E/I framework for how psychedelics change affect and the self

Winkelman MJ
Journal of Psychedelic Studies · 2026-11-06 · doi:10.1556/2054.2026.00557
Psychedelics (general)ClinicalPeer reviewedtheoretical review#E/I balance#self#affect#interoception

A theoretical article proposing that 5-HT2A-driven shifts in excitation–inhibition balance propagate across a four-level hierarchy (homeostatic–autonomic, limbic, salience network, cortical self-referential), with post-acute plasticity and 'inhibitory recalibration' stabilising altered affective dynamics.

CAVEATS Single-author narrative synthesis, not a systematic review; the framework is offered as testable, but the evidence is organised narratively.
