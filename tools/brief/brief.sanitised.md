## Core interests (highest priority)

2. Novel analysis approaches to EEG (and fMRI). Both applied to psychedelics and methods advances in general that could transfer to psychedelic or mental health work. Examples: Junheng Li's sleep-as-bifurcation paper using a feature distance metric (a method relevant to tracking the acute psychedelic experience); George Blackburne's integrated information decomposition (Phi-ID) across five psychedelics, giving a unifying account of FC and LZ entropy changes.

4. EEG task studies that answer questions about psychedelic mechanisms (therapeutic or acute): plasticity, sensory processing, E/I balance.
5. Computational modelling of EEG/fMRI data from psychedelics and other drugs: what models reveal about acute and pre/post states; same for addiction and mental health imaging. Pitch computational neuroscience for someone learning it, not a mathematician, and keep it tied to his research.
6. Addiction: ONLY (a) addiction neuroimaging (EEG, fMRI, MEG, PET etc. probing addiction mechanisms; gambling especially, any addiction broadly) and (b) psychedelic studies in addiction (trials, mechanisms, imaging). 7. Fundamental understanding of EEG/MEG signals (added 30 Oct 2026, chat): advances in what generates the signals (cellular/laminar/circuit sources, biophysical and forward models, intracranial-scalp links, what oscillations, aperiodic activity and waveform shape physiologically reflect).
